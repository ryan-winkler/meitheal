# ADHD/Productivity Panel - Phase 22 Iteration 01

## Objective
Reduce user cognitive load by aligning with industry-standard patterns and resolving visual friction points.

| Persona | Recommendation | Impact (1-5) | Effort (1-5) | Risk (1-5) | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow Coach | The ClickUp Information Architecture (IA) creates an immediate sense of familiarity and reduces the mental load of searching for metadata; adopt the Properties-Top framework fully. | 5 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Execution Coach | Title breakage is the #1 cause of broken flow state. Fixing the UI truncation so users can trust the view of what they wrote is critical. | 5 | 2 | 1 | Accept |
| Knowledge Coach | Move "Ask AI to write a description" right below the properties grid and above the Description box to match the flow shown in the target UX (ClickUp screenshot). | 4 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Focus Optimizer | Eliminate overlapping UI (Related Links dropdown clipping) as it visually signals "broken software" and disrupts focus. | 4 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Automation Coach | N/A | | | | |

## Capture/Triage/Follow-through Improvement

- **Structure and Familiarity:** Adopting ClickUp's IA radically lowers the learning curve and triage cost. Instead of scanning a dual-sidebar layout, users read naturally from top-to-bottom: Identity (Title) -> State/Metadata (Grid) -> Detail (Description) -> Execution (Subtasks/Checklists).
