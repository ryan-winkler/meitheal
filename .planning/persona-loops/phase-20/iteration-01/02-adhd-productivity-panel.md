# ADHD/Productivity Panel - Phase 20 Iteration 01

## Objective
Reduce contributor cognitive load while preserving strict architecture standards.

| Persona | Recommendation | Impact (1-5) | Effort (1-5) | Risk (1-5) | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow Coach | Auto-focus the Title `<textarea>` instantly on open for NewTaskModal to remove friction from capturing thoughts. | 5 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Execution Coach | Add subtle visual prompt (e.g. pulse or highlighting) to the Status dropdown if task represents a "stale" action. | 4 | 3 | 2 | Accept |
| Knowledge Coach | Add small inline '?' tooltips next to Pillar and Principle labels to lower cognitive entry barrier for strategic alignment. | 4 | 2 | 1 | Accept |
| Focus Optimizer | Dim the right metadata column to 50% opacity until hovered to emphasize deep work on the descriptive body. | 3 | 2 | 2 | Reject |
| Automation Coach | Implement `Cmd+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter` global listener within the modals to trigger standard save and close operations. | 5 | 2 | 1 | Accept |

## Capture/Triage/Follow-through Improvement

- **Capture Speed:** The Workflow Coach's auto-focus and the Automation Coach's `Cmd+Enter` hotkey drastically reduce the physical and cognitive time to capture and store a new task, preventing thoughts from slipping away.

## Rejected or Deferred

- (Rejected) Focus Optimizer: Dimming the right column may cause users to think the inputs are disabled or inactive, conflicting with established UI norms.
