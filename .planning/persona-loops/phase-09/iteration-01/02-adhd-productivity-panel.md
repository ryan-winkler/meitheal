# ADHD/Productivity Panel - Phase 09 Iteration 01

## Objective
Reduce search friction, context switching, and "where is that?" dead-ends.

## Recommendations

| Persona | Recommendation | Impact | Effort | Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cognitive Load Coach | Use one launcher and one keyboard shortcut so search intent never forks into two different tools | 5 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Workflow Coach | Let search jump to settings, saved views, and tasks from the same place | 5 | 2 | 1 | Accept |
| Follow-through Coach | Show matched fields so users know why a result appeared | 4 | 1 | 1 | Accept |
| Friction Auditor | Add completed/archive toggles instead of hiding work behind separate pages or syntax | 4 | 2 | 1 | Accept |
| Focus Coach | Keep HA-aware actions available from search to reduce page hopping | 4 | 1 | 1 | Accept |

## Accepted UX Heuristics
- One search intent should always map to one surface
- Users should be able to tell why a result matched
- Navigation, discovery, and quick action flows should stay in one loop
