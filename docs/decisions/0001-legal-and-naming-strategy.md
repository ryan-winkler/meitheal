# ADR 0001: Legal and Naming Strategy

## Status

Historical decision from 28 February 2026. See [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) and [business use](../../COMMERCIAL.md) for the current release.

## Decision

Use Meitheal as the product name. Keep the core independent of external code. Use published interfaces for integrations and preserve the terms of separately licensed components.
